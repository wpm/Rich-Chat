# Latin Modern fonts

`latinmodern-math.woff2` and the `lmroman12-*.woff2` files are the Latin
Modern family by the GUST e-foundry (Bogusław Jackowski and Janusz M.
Nowacki), converted to WOFF2 and distributed with the
[pulldown-latex](https://github.com/carloskiki/pulldown-latex) project.
